# Excel_Sales_Analysis_Dashboard
Analysis of sales using Excel 

Excel Sales & Profit Dashboard

Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Excel Dashboard** designed to analyze sales and profit performance across different business dimensions. The dashboard helps identify profitable products, top-performing sales representatives, country-wise profitability, customer trends, and time-based profit analysis.

The project demonstrates the use of Excel for data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and dashboard creation using charts, Pivot Tables, slicers, and KPI metrics.

images/er_Dashboard.png

Dashboard Objectives

* Analyze overall business profit performance
* Track sales representative productivity
* Identify profitable products and countries
* Compare profit trends across gender segments
* Monitor product-wise credit limits
* Visualize profit trends over time


Dashboard Insights

Product vs Profit

Analyzes which products generate the highest and lowest profits, helping identify top-performing product categories.

Sales Representative vs Quantity

Tracks the quantity sold by each sales representative to measure individual sales performance and productivity.

Country vs Profit

Compares profit contribution across different countries to identify high-performing markets.

Gender vs Profit

Analyzes customer profitability based on gender segmentation to understand purchasing behavior.

Product vs Credit Limit

Shows the relationship between products and customer credit limits for financial and sales analysis.

Date vs Profit

Visualizes profit trends over time to identify seasonal patterns and business growth.
